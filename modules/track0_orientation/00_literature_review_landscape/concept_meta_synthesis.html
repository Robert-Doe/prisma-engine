Module 0 — ConceptQualitative Systematic Review / Meta-Synthesis

In one image Meta-analysis's cousin who doesn't do math. Same instinct — combine multiple independent studies systematically into one conclusion — but the raw material is themes and interpretations, not numbers, so "combining" means building a new interpretation rather than averaging.

Meta-synthesis applies systematic-review rigor (protocol, comprehensive search, quality appraisal) to qualitative studies, then synthesizes their findings — themes, concepts, participant experiences — into new, higher-order interpretive conclusions that go beyond what any single included study claimed on its own.

What it requires

  • A systematic search and screening process, same rigor bar as a quantitative systematic review
  • Quality appraisal tools built for qualitative research (e.g. CASP qualitative checklist), not RCT-oriented ones
  • A configurative synthesis method — meta-ethnography is the best-known specific technique
Why it's not just "several summaries stapled together": the synthesis step has to produce something new — a cross-cutting theme or concept none of the individual studies stated alone — or it's functioning as a summary, not a synthesis (see the synthesis-vs-summary prerequisite).
How this connects

Numerically-pooling counterpart is Meta-analysis. Its most common specific technique is Meta-ethnography.
