Module 0 — ConceptOverview

In one image An in-flight safety card, not the aircraft manual. It compresses a large body of evidence into something a non-specialist can absorb in a few minutes, deliberately trading precision for accessibility.

"Overview" is the broadest, least methodologically specific category in the typology — used for reviews aimed at summarizing a body of evidence for a general or non-specialist audience (patients, clinicians outside the specialty, policymakers), rather than for other researchers. It sits closer to a communication genre than a research method.

What distinguishes it

  • Audience-first design — clarity and brevity outrank methodological transparency
  • Often draws on existing systematic reviews rather than primary studies, compressing them further
  • Rarely has its own distinct search or synthesis methodology — it borrows from whatever review type it's summarizing
Why it's on this map at all: mostly as a warning — "overview" is used loosely enough in practice that seeing it in a title tells you almost nothing about method. Always check what's actually inside.
How this connects

Overlaps heavily with Umbrella review — both often summarize existing reviews rather than primary studies — but an umbrella review keeps a systematic, researcher-facing methodology where an overview typically doesn't.
