Module 0 — ConceptRealist Review

In one image A mechanic explaining not just "the car works" or "the car doesn't work," but exactly which part, under which conditions, causes it to start or stall — the wiring diagram behind the verdict, not just the verdict.

A realist review (realist synthesis) asks not "does this intervention work?" but "what works, for whom, under what circumstances, and through what mechanism, and why?" It's built around context-mechanism-outcome (CMO) configurations: the same intervention can succeed in one context and fail in another, and the review's job is to explain that pattern rather than average it away.

What makes it distinct

  • Theory-driven — starts from candidate program theories about how and why something might work
  • Deliberately includes diverse evidence types (quantitative, qualitative, even grey literature) if they illuminate mechanism
  • Iterative search — new searches are triggered by emerging theory, not fixed upfront like a systematic review's protocol
Why this isn't "just a critical review with extra steps": a realist review's CMO framework is a specific, disciplined analytic structure — every claim has to be anchored to a context and a mechanism, not just argued for narratively.
How this connects

Shares its narrow, configurative, explanation-seeking approach with Critical review, but grounds its explanations in systematically extracted context-mechanism-outcome data rather than argument alone.
