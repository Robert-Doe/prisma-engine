Module 0 — ConceptBibliometric Review / Citation Analysis

In one image Studying a city by analyzing its road network and traffic flow — who connects to whom, which intersections everyone passes through — instead of walking into any of the buildings to see what's inside.

A bibliometric review analyzes the literature itself as structured data — citation counts, co-authorship networks, co-citation clusters, keyword co-occurrence — rather than reading and synthesizing what individual studies actually found. Its unit of analysis is the paper as a node in a network, not the paper's content.

What it can and can't tell you

Can reveal

  • Which papers/authors are most influential (by citation count)
  • How a field clusters into sub-communities (co-citation analysis)
  • How a field's vocabulary and focus has shifted over time

Cannot reveal

  • Whether a highly-cited finding is actually correct
  • What any individual study's methodology or quality was
  • Nuance lost when citation counts get treated as a quality proxy
How this connects

Uses the same corpus-building machinery as Mapping review, but analyzes citation structure instead of study characteristics. Often used alongside Scoping review to identify which papers deserve closer reading.
